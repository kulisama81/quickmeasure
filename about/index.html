About

Sourced Calc is a free site of small calculators. We use official numbers. We do not sell anything here.

Each calculator page links the official source and shows the date we last opened that page. If the official number changes, our page can be wrong until we open it again.

These are estimates, not advice. We are not a contractor, a lender, or a tax advisor. Check the linked official source before you decide anything.
