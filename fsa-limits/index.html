How much you can put in a health FSA this year (2026)

Pick whether your plan lets unused health FSA money carry to next year. We look up the official yearly dollar. Not tax advice.

Whether unused money can carry to next year is required. How much you plan to set aside from pay is optional.

Only use the $680 leftover number if your plan lets unused money move to next year. Many plans do not. If you are not sure, check your plan paperwork.

What this number is

  • Under this dollar, you are within the IRS cap for what you set aside from pay; over it, the IRS does not let that salary reduction go higher for the year.
  • The official 2026 cap for what you set aside from pay is $3,400.
  • If the plan lets unused money carry to next year, the max leftover is $680. Only use that $680 if the plan allows it. That leftover is unused money, not extra you can set aside from this year's pay.
  • This page is only the health FSA set-aside cap and that leftover max. We do not look up dependent-care FSA, transit, parking, or other fringe dollars here.
