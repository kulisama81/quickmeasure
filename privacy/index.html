Privacy

Sourced Calc does not have accounts. You do not sign in.

What you type into a calculator stays in your browser. We do not send those numbers to a server, and we do not store them.

We use Google Analytics to count visits. It can tell which pages people open. We do not send it the numbers you type into a calculator.

Cloudflare serves the pages. A request shows which page you opened. It does not include the numbers you type.

We do not sell data. We do not show ads today.
