Assumptions

  • Wall area = 2 × (length + width) × height. Ceiling and floor are not included.
  • Default coverage is 350 sq ft/gal, within the SuperPaint PDS 350–400 range at 4 mils wet. Edit the field to match the can.
  • Each door subtracts 20 sq ft (3 ft × 6 ft 8 in typical interior door opening). This is a typical opening, not manufacturer coverage.
  • Each window subtracts 15 sq ft (3 ft × 5 ft typical opening). This is a typical opening, not manufacturer coverage.
  • Openings that exceed wall area are clamped to zero remaining paint area.
  • Purchase gallons are rounded up. The raw (unrounded) number is also shown.

Trim, texture, waste, primer, and odd walls will move the number. This is an estimate, not a contractor quote.
