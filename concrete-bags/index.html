Concrete bag estimator

Rectangular slab. Length and width in feet, thickness in inches, then premix bags. Yields from the official Quikrete and Sakrete pages below. Estimate, not a quote.

All three dimensions are required. Bags are rounded up.

Common slabs are 4 inches. Confirm your spec.

Bag yields we use

  • 0.60 cubic feet per 80 lb bag
  • 0.45 cubic feet per 60 lb bag
  • 0.30 cubic feet per 40 lb bag

Those figures are the published Quikrete 1101 and Sakrete High-Strength yields in the sources table. Always read the bag you are buying; mix, moisture, and waste change the count. Volume = length × width × (thickness in inches ÷ 12).

Rounded up. This is an estimate, not a contractor quote, structural design, or a mix spec.
