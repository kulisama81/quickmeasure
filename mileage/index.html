How much the IRS says one mile is worth (2026)

Pick the day of the trip and what kind of trip it was. We look up the official cents-per-mile rate. Estimate, not tax advice.

When the trip was and what kind of trip are required. Miles are optional.

The official rate depends on the day of the trip. 2026 has one rate for January through June and another for July through December.
Business or self-employed is work driving. Charity is volunteer driving for a charity. Medical is driving for medical care. Moving is only for active-duty military — the IRS page does not treat a regular household move as this rate.

What this number is

  • This is how much the IRS says one mile is worth.
  • Business or self-employed is 72.5¢ a mile from January 1 through June 30, 2026, and 76¢ a mile from July 1 through December 31, 2026.
  • Charity is 14¢ a mile for both halves of 2026.
  • Medical and moving for military are 20.5¢ a mile from January 1 through June 30, 2026, and 23.5¢ a mile from July 1 through December 31, 2026. The IRS page labels moving as military only. A regular household move is not this rate.
  • We do not have official rates here for any year other than 2026.

This is an estimate, not tax advice or a quote. Whether a trip qualifies is still between you and the IRS.
